## Fixed
- **TCGplayer prices for old holos:** TCGdex renamed two of TCGplayer's price kinds ("1st-edition-holofoil" and "unlimited-holofoil"), so 1st edition and unlimited holo copies of older cards (Neo Genesis, Jungle, Base Set 2…) got no TCGplayer price and fell back to Cardmarket's ("≈"). The app now reads both the new and the old names.
- Note: on 30 October 2026 TCGdex briefly sent no TCGplayer prices at all. The app keeps the prices it already has meanwhile; new ones arrive by themselves once TCGdex sends them again.

Install `PokeTrader-1.21.apk` (64-bit ARM phones) or `PokeTrader-1.21-universal.apk` (any device). Installs over 1.20 and keeps your cards.

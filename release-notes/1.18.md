## Fixed
- **TCGplayer prices are fetched once:** when several price updates start at the same moment (opening the app, coming back online, the background update), TCGplayer's prices could be fetched more than once. The check "is it due?" now happens when the update's turn comes, so the later ones see the fresh prices and skip.

Install `PokeTrader-1.18.apk` (64-bit ARM phones) or `PokeTrader-1.18-universal.apk` (any device).

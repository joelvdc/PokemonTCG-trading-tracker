## New
- **Settings → Price source:** value your cards with Cardmarket (as before) or TCGplayer. The collection total, sorting, the value filter and trades follow it. TCGplayer has one market price per card version, for English prints (normal, holo, reverse holo, 1st edition); other versions and Japanese prints keep Cardmarket's price, marked "≈".
- **Compare them:** the collection value screen shows both sources side by side; tap one to see its total and chart (each source's chart fills in from today).
- **Stats:** "Value by price source" (for the whole collection or one binder) and "Europe or the US?", the cards whose price differs most between Cardmarket and TCGplayer.
- **Card page:** "Prices elsewhere" shows TCGplayer's price for that version, with a link to it.
- TCGplayer's prices come with TCGdex's card data, refreshed with the daily price update (about a minute per 1,000 cards), and are converted from dollars with the daily exchange rates.

Install `PokeTrader-1.16.apk` (64-bit ARM phones) or `PokeTrader-1.16-universal.apk` (any device).

## New
- **Sort by how prices moved:** "Change in %" and "Change in money" in the sort window, biggest rise or biggest fall first, plus the ready-made "Rising lately" and "Falling lately". The change is per card: Cardmarket's trend price against its 30-day average. Cards without a 30-day average go last. "Rising lately" and "Falling lately" on the value screen now count per card too.
- **Select several cards:** press and hold a card (or ⋮ → Select cards), then tap more. The bar at the top shows how many and their value, with Move to binder, Add to a trade and Delete, and under ⋮: Edit condition, language, price…, Share as a list, Copy as a list, Export as CSV and Select all shown. Everything can be undone.
- **Swipe cards** in the list and compact views: right moves a card to a binder, left adds it to a trade. Settings → Swiping cards chooses what each direction does (also one copy more or less, or onto the wishlist).
- **Arrange binders:** ⋮ → Arrange binders… sets the order of the binder bar and of every binder list.
- **Paid and worth** on the value screen: what you paid for the cards with a purchase price against what they're worth now, with your best and worst buys.
- **Wishlist: To buy, Ordered, Received.** Tick "Ordered" on a card, or swipe it right: To buy → Ordered → Received → into the collection (choosing the version, condition, binder and what you paid). Swipe left to delete. Received cards stay as a record until you clear them, by hand or after 1–12 months (Settings). ⋮ has Mark all ordered cards as received, Mark the cards I now own as received and Clear received cards. Ordered and received cards are left out when you share the wishlist.
- **Price alerts:** open a wishlist card and fill in "Notify me at or below". You get a notification when the card (for "any version" cards, its cheapest version) costs that much or less, checked after each price update. Settings → Wishlist price alerts chooses which Cardmarket price counts (lowest listing by default).
- **Add to the wishlist offline:** without internet, "Add card" on the wishlist suggests names from Cardmarket's card list on the phone. The card's set, number and picture are filled in once you're back online.
- **Prices in the currency you paid in:** every price field (what you paid, an agreed price, the alert level) has a € / kr. / $ button. The price is kept in that currency: 300 kr. stays 300 kr., whatever the exchange rate does. Switching the button converts the amount. When cards join a stack whose price is in another currency, the app asks which currency to keep. CSV backups get a "Purchase currency" column.
- **Sync between your phones with Nextcloud:** Settings → Sync with Nextcloud keeps the collection, binders, wishlist, trades, scans and settings the same on both phones. Log in through the browser, then choose the folder for the sync file (or make a new one; not the MTG Trader folder).
- **Backups and restore points:** Settings → Backups. A backup every day when something changed, and before big changes (the first sync, CSV imports, deleting selected cards, restoring). "Back up now", save to or restore from a file, and a preview of what differs before restoring.

## Changed
- The scanner's list jumps back to the newest card each time one is added.
- A card that moves from one binder to another keeps what you paid for it; joining another stack gives the average paid.

Install `PokeTrader-1.20.apk` (64-bit ARM phones) or `PokeTrader-1.20-universal.apk` (any device). Installs over 1.19 and keeps your cards. Update both phones before you start syncing.

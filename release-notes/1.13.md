## New
- **Collection stats:** tap the pie-chart button next to the collection total (or ⋮ → Collection stats). For all cards or one binder, counted by cards or by value: totals (and what you paid against what those cards are worth now), rarity, **set completion** (how many of each set's numbers you own, plus secret rares), era (Base, Scarlet & Violet, Mega Evolution…), special Pokémon (ex, V, VMAX, GX, Mega, Radiant…), the Pokémon you have most of, price ranges, top sets, version, print, language, condition, binders, cards added per month and the most valuable cards. Tap a line to see those cards in the collection.

## Scanner
- **Mega Evolution era:** Japanese set codes like "M2a J 015/193" are read correctly (the "J" mark used to be taken for the set code), and Korean and Chinese Mega prints are found too.
- 30th Celebration's "30C EN" set code is read.

## Other
- Rarity sorting knows the newest rarities: Futuristic Rare sorts with the hyper rares, Pikachu Rare above Rare.
- 30th Celebration cards show no price yet: TCGdex hasn't linked them to Cardmarket, and the app doesn't guess.

Install `PokeTrader-1.13.apk` (64-bit ARM phones) or `PokeTrader-1.13-universal.apk` (any device).

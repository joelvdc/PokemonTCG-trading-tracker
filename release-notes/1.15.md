## New
- **Settings → Currency:** show every price in euros, Danish kroner or US dollars. Cardmarket's euro prices are converted with the European Central Bank's daily rates, which the app keeps for when you're offline (Settings shows the rate and its date).
- Everything follows the choice: the collection, card pages, search, trades, the value chart, stats and the wishlist. The value filter and the purchase and agreed prices you type use it too.
- The app still keeps prices in euros, and CSV files are unchanged.

Install `PokeTrader-1.15.apk` (64-bit ARM phones) or `PokeTrader-1.15-universal.apk` (any device).
